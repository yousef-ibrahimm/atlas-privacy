Atlas — Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Atlas is a personal application with one user, its owner, Yousef Ibrahim. No one else can sign in to it.

Google user data Atlas accesses

  • Google Calendar: the owner's calendars and events, to list, create, update and delete events at his request.
  • Google Tasks: the owner's task lists and tasks, to list, create, update and complete tasks at his request.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests and to send him reminders. The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted on the owner's own machine. Calendar and task data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

To carry out a request, the text of the request and relevant event or task details may be processed by a language model through the owner's own model provider account.

Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
